💡 Quick Concept Cheat Sheet (For Your Reference):

Runway vs Taxiway Taxiways are the slow side roads. Runways are the high-speed tracks where giant jets land at 150 mph.
Tug (Truck #42) The small, heavy tractor that pushes and tows big airplanes around the airport.
ATC (Air Traffic Control) The airport traffic police sitting up in the tall control tower.
"Hold Short" Airport slang for: "STOP BEFORE THE LINE! DO NOT CROSS THE RUNWAY!"
